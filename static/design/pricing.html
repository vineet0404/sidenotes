Questions

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. You keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for.

What happens to my files if I stop paying?

Your library keeps your files until the retention period ends. After that they are deleted.

Do you train a model on my documents?

No. Files are used only to answer your questions.
