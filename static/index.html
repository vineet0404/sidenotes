How it works

  1. 1

    Upload your files

    Add up to 5 PDFs or notes. Sidenote reads the text and splits it into passages.

  2. 2

    Ask a question

    Type it the way you would ask a person. English, Hindi and Hinglish all work.

  3. 3

    Check the source

    Select a numbered citation to open the page, with the matching sentence highlighted.

Ask in the language you think in

Your documents can be in English and your question in Hindi or Hinglish. The answer comes back in the language you asked in, with the same citations.

पौधे भोजन कैसे बनाते हैं?
