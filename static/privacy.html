Privacy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Sidenote answers questions about files you upload. This page says exactly what happens to those files, your questions and any feedback you send.

Your files and questions

  • Files you upload are read, split into passages and kept in the memory of the server for your session only. They are not written to a database or to disk.
  • Your session, with its files and chat, is deleted automatically after 2 hours without activity. Remove a single file with the × next to it, or press New chat to clear everything now.
  • We do not log the text of your files or your questions.
  • There is no account. Your browser stores a random session ID, and your theme choice, in local storage. Neither is linked to your name or email.

The AI model

To write an answer, Sidenote sends your question, your recent chat and the most relevant passages from your files to the AI model that runs the service. That is the only place your document text goes. Do not upload anything you are not allowed to share, such as other people's confidential records.

Feedback

  • Helpful and Not helpful record only the rating, the model name, the number of sources and the length of the answer.
  • If you choose Not helpful, we also store the reason you pick and any comment you type.
  • Your question and the answer are sent only if you tick the box that says so. Your files are never sent.

Technical data

To limit abuse, the server briefly counts requests per IP address in memory. It is not stored with your files or questions. The hosting provider or proxy in front of the site may keep standard access logs, such as IP address and time of request, under its own policy.

Your choices

You can delete your files at any time, clear your browser's local storage to drop the session ID, or ask us about feedback you sent.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
